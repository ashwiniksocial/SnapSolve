- exact_defective_text
- reason
- expected_correction

calculation, logical contradiction or directly verifiable curriculum fact.

do not classify it as CONFIRMED_DEFECT.

Routing:

- second reviewer confirms the same defect with complete evidence
  → CONFIRMED_DEFECT

- second reviewer disagrees, changes the allegation, or provides incomplete
  evidence
  → REVIEWER_UNCERTAINTY

Do not send possible defects to human review by default.
